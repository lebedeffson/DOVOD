# DOVOD Q1 research 2026

This branch contains the reproducible post-conference Q1 research expansion under `research/q1_2026/`. The frozen `main` short-paper evidence is intentionally left unchanged.

The package contains two separate research lines: (A) identifiability and certified contextual repair of learned action-model applicability decisions; and (B) identifiability-aware Bayesian evidence acquisition with persistent source orientation/reliability and an exact evidence-count Bellman quotient.

Run `cd research/q1_2026 && python -m pytest -q` to verify the local core.
