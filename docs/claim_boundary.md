# Claim boundary

Supported by the frozen experiments:

- one-sided falsification of unary, unconditional, same-state prerequisite candidates;
- independent-recording calibration and carrier-strength analysis;
- finite-model selection between physical-state queries and semantic/procedure review;
- controlled noisy-source Bayesian stress tests;
- algorithmic exact-planning scalability measurements.

Not established by the current repository:

- authoritative mechanical/SOP truth for positive prerequisite claims;
- conditional, conjunctive, disjunctive, resource or causal prerequisites in general;
- measured live sensor/expert error distributions;
- repeated/correlated/adversarial misinformation in deployment;
- human benefit, workload reduction or physical safety improvement;
- real OpenXR/headset timing or energy savings.
